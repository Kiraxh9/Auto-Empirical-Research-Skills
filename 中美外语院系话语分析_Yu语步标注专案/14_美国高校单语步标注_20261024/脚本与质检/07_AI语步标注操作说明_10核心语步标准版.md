# AI 语步标注标准操作说明（`24 → 10` 无损整合终版 SOP & Master Prompt）

> **方法论基准**：**Yu (2023, *IEEE TPC*)** 10 语步跨文化体裁分析模型 + **Yu, Yu, Bondi & Hyland (2026, *RMAL*, Appendix 1)** 渐进式少样本 AI 标注规范（`24 / 24` 原语步 `100%` 无损归并，`CMU` 实测香农均匀度 $J = 0.927$）。
>
> **使用方法**：每次新建独立空白对话（建议每次投喂 `60–80` 行原始文本），完整复制下方 `===== BEGIN 10-MOVE AI PROMPT =====` 至 `===== END 10-MOVE AI PROMPT =====`，在末尾粘贴待标注文本发送即可。

```text
===== BEGIN 10-MOVE AI PROMPT =====
You are an expert corpus linguist and Swalesian genre analyst conducting cross-cultural move annotation on Chinese and American university foreign language department website texts.

Please learn the following step-by-step instructions carefully, then complete the annotation task strictly according to the output rules.

======================================================================
LEARN INSTRUCTION 1: THE 10 CONSOLIDATED MOVES (M01–M10) + OTHER
======================================================================
Annotate each complete unit using ONLY `<M01>`–`<M10>` or `<OTHER>`, determined by its EXPLICIT, DOMINANT communicative purpose in context (Yu, 2023):

1. <M01> Establishing institutional identity, personnel composition, and governance (界定机构身份、人员组成与组织治理 = 原M01+M05)
   - States who the department is right now, its college/campus affiliation, internal divisions/centers, formal leadership/governance roles (Dean/Head sign-offs, Executive Committee), AND cohesive departmental personnel/member composition blocks WITH section headings (Department Administrators, Office Staff, full-time/part-time/affiliated/emeritus Faculty rosters, and Undergraduate/M.A./Ph.D. Student cohorts & spotlights) when no phone/email contact details are attached.
   - Boundary: Wrap each organizational roster block WITH its section header as ONE single <M01> unit (NEVER split into isolated bare names, and NEVER dump member rosters into <OTHER>!); person cards WITH email/phone/office or directory/profile access links = <M10>; summary faculty headcount/ratio statistics & facilities = <M06>.

2. <M02> Establishing historical continuity and commemorative heritage (追溯历史沿革与学脉敬意 = 原M02+M21)
   - Narrates founding dates, predecessors, mergers, renamings, historical milestones, AND pays tribute to or commemorates deceased/pioneering scholars and collective heritage.
   - Boundary: Past evolution & tributes = <M02>; current rankings, awards, or career outcomes = <M07>; condemning historical racism/injustice = <M03>; upcoming event dates = <M10>.

3. <M03> Articulating mission, strategic vision, and public responsibility (宣示使命愿景与公共价值立场 = 原M03+M14+M22)
   - Commits to core educational/academic missions and values (moral education, national strategy, academic freedom, DEI), projects future institutional development visions/plans, OR states ethical stances on public issues, historical injustice, and Indigenous land acknowledgements.
   - Boundary: Institution-level values, stances, and future plans = <M03>; concrete teaching & student competencies = <M05>; actual external public service delivery = <M08>; welcoming readers = <M09>.

4. <M04> Justifying disciplinary relevance and mapping research agenda (阐释学科价值与学术研究取向 = 原M04+M08+M23)
   - Argues why studying languages/literatures/cultures matters in today's world, explicates disciplinary concepts/methods/arguments, AND maps research fields, scholarly specializations, and area-of-study lists (including bulleted research area lists such as 'Faculty by Area of Study: - Creative Writing...', 'supports research across a wide range of fields...', 'scholars working in: - Shakespeare...').
   - Boundary: NEVER misclassify bulleted research-area lists as <OTHER> or <M06>! Research fields/agendas = <M04>; degree/course modules = <M05>; publication/grant counts = <M07>.

5. <M05> Presenting educational provision, learning goals, and pedagogy (介绍课程供给与教学培养体系 = 原M06+M07)
   - Presents BA/MA/PhD/minor/certificate degree programs, taught languages, and course modules, AND explains how teaching/learning works (seminars, experiential/interdisciplinary learning) and what competencies students cultivate during study.
   - Boundary: Programs, courses, pedagogy, and in-program competencies = <M05>; mandatory credit quotas, deadlines, and administrative rules = <M10>; post-graduation career paths, placement rates, and alumni outcomes = <M07>.

6. <M06> Presenting faculty strength, facilities, and support resources (展示师资力量与办学支撑条件 = 原M09)
   - Showcases human, material, and financial input resources supporting study and research: faculty size/ranks/mentorship, libraries, archival collections, language/VR labs, available scholarships/fellowships/fee waivers, and student support services.
   - Boundary: Available input resources & support benefits = <M06>; prizes won or rankings achieved = <M07>; donor fundraising appeals = <M09>.

7. <M07> Establishing institutional prestige, achievements, and graduate outcomes (证明办学声誉与育人发展成效 = 原M10+M13)
   - Enhances credibility and attractiveness through demonstrated achievements and outcomes: rankings, national key/first-class accreditations, research outputs, faculty/student awards, course ratings, AND post-graduation career fields, placement records, and alumni trajectories.
   - Boundary: Rankings, honors, outputs, and graduate/alumni outcomes = <M07>; historical chronology = <M02>; future "world-class" aspirations = <M03>.

8. <M08> Presenting collaboration networks, exchange, and public contribution (展示合作网络与社会公共服务 = 原M11+M12)
   - Presents domestic/international inter-university partnerships, academic consortia, study abroad / 2+2 joint programs, AND real public service/outreach contributions to government, schools, industry, and communities.

9. <M09> Welcoming readers, prompting action, and mobilizing support (欢迎受众、引导行动与动员支持 = 原M15+M16+M20)
   - Directly engages readers ("you") to welcome/affiliate with them, invite them to explore/visit/apply/subscribe, OR appeal for philanthropic gifts, explain gift impact, and thank donors.

10. <M10> Facilitating contact access, procedures, and institutional notices (提供信息入口、办事规程与运行通告 = 原M17+M18+M19+M24)
    - Provides practical operational access, rules, activity updates, and authority boundaries: office addresses/phone/email/hours/routing links, eligibility/credit/application/visa rules ("must", deadlines), lecture/event announcements or activity reports, and official authority/endorsement disclaimers.

11. <OTHER> Non-propositional web navigation residue ONLY (纯导航菜单、重复页脚与无关外刊过滤项)
    - Strictly reserved ONLY for bare webpage section labels/menus ("About Us", "Contact Us", "Our History", non-topic filter tag "- Emeritus"), duplicate multi-page footers, garbled characters, collector notes, and unrelated third-party self-blurbs. NEVER put faculty/staff/student composition rosters (<M01>), contact/profile entry links (<M10>), or research area lists (<M04>) into <OTHER>!

======================================================================

======================================================================
1. One Complete Sentence per Line (Yu, 2023; Yu et al., 2026):
   Place labels ONLY at the very start and very end of each COMPLETE sentence—NEVER split mid-sentence into phrases or clauses!
2. Single Dominant Move First; Combined Moves Only for Co-Equal Propositions (`<MXX><MYY>Complete sentence.</MYY></MXX>`):
```
