# AI 语步标注标准操作说明（精炼 10 核心语步 + `OTHER` 终版 SOP & Master Prompt）

> **方法论基准**：**Yu (2023, *IEEE TPC*)** 10 语步跨文化体裁分析模型 + **Yu, Yu, Bondi & Hyland (2026, *RMAL*, Appendix 1)** 渐进式少样本 AI 标注规范（两两判别边界较 24 语步减少 **83.7%**，专为双大模型高一致性盲标设计）。
>
> **使用方法**：每次新建独立空白对话（建议分块每次投喂 `60–80` 行原始文本），完整复制下方 `===== BEGIN 10-MOVE AI PROMPT =====` 至 `===== END 10-MOVE AI PROMPT =====`，在最末尾粘贴待标注文本发送即可。

```text
===== BEGIN 10-MOVE AI PROMPT =====
You are an expert corpus linguist and Swalesian genre analyst conducting cross-cultural move annotation on Chinese and American university foreign language department "About Us" introductory texts.

Please learn the following step-by-step instructions carefully, then complete the annotation task strictly according to the output rules.

======================================================================
LEARN INSTRUCTION 1: THE 10 CORE RHETORICAL MOVES + OTHER SCHEMA
======================================================================
Annotate each complete unit using ONLY the following 11 XML tags (`<M01>`–`<M10>`, or `<OTHER>`), determined by the sentence's EXPLICIT, DOMINANT communicative purpose in context (Yu, 2023):

1. <M01> Establishing institutional identity and governance (界定机构定位与治理架构)
   - Answers: Who is this department right now, what college/campus does it belong to, what internal departments/divisions/research centers does it comprise, and who holds formal leadership/governance roles (including Dean/Head sign-offs `Name + Official Title`)?
   - Boundary: Current governance & center names = <M01>; faculty headcounts/facilities = <M07>; historical origin/renaming = <M02>; bare webpage breadcrumbs or raw directory lists = <OTHER>.

2. <M02> Tracing historical continuity and heritage (追溯历史沿革与学脉传承)
   - Answers: Where did this department come from, how did it evolve chronologically (founding, mergers, renamings, historical degree milestones), and what pioneering scholars/traditions shaped its heritage?
   - Boundary: Chronological past narrative = <M02>; current degree/course offerings today = <M04>; standalone obituary news posts ("In Memoriam") = <OTHER>.

3. <M03> Articulating mission, values, and strategic vision (宣示使命愿景与价值立场)
   - Answers: What core mission, motto, ethical values (e.g., moral education, academic freedom, DEI, social justice, Indigenous land acknowledgement), or forward-looking strategic vision ("Looking ahead...", "aim to ensure that X continues to...") guides the institution?
   - Boundary: Abstract institutional ethos, ethical stances, and future vision = <M03>; concrete teaching methods, student skills, or career outcomes = <M05>; courses covering race/gender topics = <M04>.

4. <M04> Presenting degree programs and curricular provision (介绍学位体系与课程供给)
   - Answers: What BA/MA/PhD/postdoc degree programs, majors, minors, combination majors, certificates, languages, and courses are currently offered to learners?
   - Boundary: Inventory of degree programs, languages, and course topics = <M04>; how teaching cultivates student abilities or prepares careers = <M05>.

5. <M05> Explaining pedagogy, competencies, and graduate outcomes (阐明育人目标、教学模式与毕业发展)
   - Answers: How does the department teach and mentor students (seminars, co-curricular journals/workshops, interdisciplinary study), what competencies do students develop (linguistic, critical thinking, intercultural skills), and what career paths, graduate school placements, or alumni outcomes do graduates achieve?
   - Boundary: Covers the entire student cultivation-to-career trajectory (`in-program learning + post-graduation career/alumni outcomes`) = <M05>.

6. <M06> Explicating disciplinary relevance and research agenda (阐释学科价值与研究专长)
   - Answers: Why does studying languages/literatures/cultures matter in today's world ("More and more, the world needs..."), and what scholarly fields, periods, regions, theoretical questions, and interdisciplinary clusters do THIS department's faculty actively research?
   - Boundary: Must describe general disciplinary relevance or THIS target department's own research agenda = <M06>; external library/consortium collection coverage = <M07>; publication/grant counts = <M08>.

7. <M07> Presenting faculty strength and support resources (展示师资队伍与支撑资源)
   - Answers: What human, material, archival, and financial resources support teaching and research (faculty scale, rank ratios, student-teacher ratio, libraries, archival collections, language/VR labs, institutional scholarships, fellowships, fee waivers, travel grants, and student support services)?
   - Boundary: Input resources & available scholarships/benefits (including "enjoy exclusive eligibility for...") = <M07>; rankings, awards won, or output counts = <M08>; asking donors/alumni to give money = <M10>.

8. <M08> Establishing academic reputation and achievements (证明学术声誉与办学成就)
   - Answers: What rankings, national key/first-class accreditations, research outputs, major prizes (e.g., Pulitzer Prize, Phi Beta Kappa, award-winning faculty), student course ratings, historical "first in the country" distinctions, or cumulative conference-hosting achievements prove institutional excellence?
   - Boundary: Cumulative honors & undated conference summaries ("successfully hosted many international conferences") = <M08>; single dated upcoming event notices = <OTHER>.

9. <M09> Presenting external partnerships, exchange, and public outreach (拓展合作交流与公共服务)
   - Answers: How does the department connect across institutional and national borders—through inter-university partnerships, academic consortia, study abroad / student exchange / 2+2 joint programs, OR through public outreach serving government, K–12 schools, industry, and society?

10. <M10> Engaging readers, facilitating access, and mobilizing support (建立受众互动、联络与支持关系)
    - Answers: How does the text directly address readers ("you") to welcome them, invite them to explore/apply/connect, provide office addresses/phone/email/hours coordinates, or mobilize philanthropic alumni/donor gifts and thank donors ("Give Today", "Make a gift", "Through the generosity of...")?

11. <OTHER> Out-of-genre web artifacts and non-propositional residue (非简介异质子体裁与网页残片过滤项)
    - Use <OTHER> whenever none of <M01>–<M10> applies:
      (a) Isolated webpage menus, section headings without propositional content ("About Us", "Contact Us", "Our History", "Meet the Department", "View all individual faculty profiles", filter lists with "- Emeritus"), breadcrumbs, HTML/image-alt residue, garbled text, collector notes, and duplicate footers;
      (b) Raw scraped faculty/staff/student directory rosters consisting only of name/title/major cards without prose sentences;
      (c) Out-of-genre web pages accidentally scraped into the file (mandatory travel/insurance/handbook regulations, dated single-event news feeds, standalone "In Memoriam" obituaries, legal disclaimers, technical lab formulas) OR third-party partner self-blurbs where "we" refers to an external entity rather than the university department.

======================================================================
LEARN INSTRUCTION 2: COMPLETE-SENTENCE ANNOTATION & COMBINED MOVES
======================================================================
1. One Complete Sentence per Line (Yu, 2023; Yu et al., 2026):
   Place labels ONLY at the very start and very end of each COMPLETE sentence—NEVER split mid-sentence into phrases or clauses!
2. Single Dominant Move First:
   If brief phrases appear merely as subordinate modifiers supporting one clear main purpose, assign ONLY the single dominant move:
   <M10>We warmly invite you to visit our world by exploring our many course offerings, two majors and eight minors and other amazing opportunities to engage with the rich diversity of languages and cultures around us.</M10>
3. Combined Moves Only for Co-Equal Propositions (`<MXX><MYY>Complete sentence.</MYY></MXX>`):
   When a single complete sentence genuinely gives co-equal weight to two distinct moves, wrap the intact complete sentence with nested tags:
   <M05><M06>Our research and teaching promotes language learning, deepens cultural understanding through literary and cultural studies and highlights cutting-edge technology-enhanced language learning.</M06></M05>
   <M02><M08>In 1958, the BS in Technical Writing & Editing, the first degree of its kind in the country, was added as a possible major within MMCC under the initiative of Erwin Steinberg.</M08></M02>
```
